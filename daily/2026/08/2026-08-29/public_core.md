## 2. Market And Event Delta

### Hormuz / shipping
Seven commodity vessels crossed Hormuz in the preliminary Thursday count, including one VLGC; the count was below both the prior day's 17 and the 10-day average of 15. **Analysis:** physical flows remain volatile and below recent norms. A durable rise in vessel counts is the critical falsifier/confirmation variable for any normalization thesis. [E01](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/shipping-traffic-via-strait-hormuz-slips-below-10-day-average-data-shows-2026-08-28/)

### LNG / Europe
QatarEnergy's Edison force majeure now extends to early November. Five further cargoes scheduled from late September to early November were canceled, bringing the total to 29 cargoes / 3.8 bcm. Edison said 21 replacement cargoes, about 2 bcm, had already been sourced at Adriatic LNG. Germany simultaneously reported storage at 51.64% and improving injections, attributing this partly to a better winter-summer spread and weaker Asian LNG competition after the regional heatwave eased. **Analysis:** Europe is replacing disrupted term supply, but the marginal cargo balance has temporarily become less adversarial. [E03](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/qatarenergy-cancels-gas-deliveries-italys-edison-until-early-november-2026-08-28/) [E04](https://www.reuters.com/business/germany-says-gas-storage-injections-pick-up-lng-market-conditions-improve-2026-08-28/)

### Refined products / Russia
Reuters sources put late-August Russian gasoline production near 80,000 t/d versus estimated summer demand of 115,000 t/d after outages at major refineries. Seaborne petroleum-product imports from Asia were expected around 270,000 tonnes in August and Belarus gasoline imports around 150,000 tonnes. **Analysis:** refinery outages are converting geopolitical risk into product-specific scarcity and import pull. [E05](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/russias-gasoline-output-drops-70-domestic-demand-late-august-after-drone-attacks-sources-say-2026-08-28/)
